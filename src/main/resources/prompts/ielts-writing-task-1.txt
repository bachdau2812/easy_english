TASK ACHIEVEMENT

Assess how fully, appropriately, accurately, and relevantly the response fulfils Task 1.

For charts, graphs, tables, and mixed visuals:
- Check accurate identification of what the visual shows.
- Require a clear and accurate overview of the main features.
- Check selection of important trends, contrasts, highs/lows, similarities, or other salient features.
- Reward meaningful grouping/comparison rather than mechanical value listing.
- Check values, units, categories, rankings, and directions of change against the authoritative analysis.
- Do not require every minor detail.

For processes:
- Check major stages, sequence, inputs/outputs, transformations, and cyclical features where relevant.
- Penalize material sequencing or transformation errors.
- Do not reward speculation beyond the diagram.

For maps/plans:
- Check major spatial features and changes.
- Check additions, removals, replacements, expansions, relocations, and before/after comparisons.
- Penalize material spatial inaccuracies.

For mixed visuals:
- Check that all major components receive meaningful coverage.

TASK ACHIEVEMENT BAND GATES

Band 8 is eligible only if ALL are true:
- overview is clear, accurate, and captures the dominant features;
- all major visual components are addressed;
- key features are skilfully selected and grouped;
- comparisons are meaningful;
- supporting data/details are accurate and sufficient;
- there is no material error in a main trend, ranking, unit, category, direction, comparison, stage, or map change;
- no important key feature is omitted or substantially misrepresented;
- omissions/lapses are genuinely occasional.

If any condition fails, do not award Band 8.

Band 7 is eligible only if ALL are true:
- a clear and substantially accurate overview is present;
- main trends/differences/stages/changes are identified;
- important features are selected rather than merely listed;
- support is relevant and generally accurate;
- comparisons/grouping are present where needed;
- inaccuracies and omissions are limited;
- no major visual component is effectively ignored.

Conservative Task Achievement caps:
- no identifiable overview -> normally no higher than Band 5;
- overview materially inaccurate or missing a dominant feature -> normally no higher than Band 6;
- major trend/direction/ranking/unit wrong -> Band 8 not eligible;
- several material factual errors -> normally no higher than Band 6;
- mostly mechanical listing with little grouping/comparison -> normally no higher than Band 6;
- one major component ignored in a mixed task -> normally no higher than Band 6.

Factual errors primarily affect Task Achievement. Do not automatically lower LR or GRA unless there is an independent language problem.

The task requires at least 150 words. Do not apply a fixed penalty solely for underlength; judge its effect on coverage and evidence of language ability. Do not double-penalize.

COHERENCE AND COHESION

Assess logical organization, progression, grouping, sequencing, paragraphing, reference/substitution, and cohesive devices.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- the response is easy to follow throughout;
- paragraph purpose/grouping is consistently clear;
- progression is logical within and between paragraphs;
- cohesion is natural and unobtrusive;
- reference/substitution are effective;
- there is no recurring mechanical linking, unclear reference, abrupt progression, or weak paragraph focus.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- progression is clearly maintained;
- paragraphing is logical and purposeful;
- cohesive devices show some flexibility;
- referencing is generally clear;
- lapses are limited.

If organization is understandable but noticeably mechanical, linkers are repetitive, or paragraph focus/progression is uneven, normally consider Band 6.
Paragraphing alone is never evidence of Band 7 or 8.

LEXICAL RESOURCE

Assess range, flexibility, precision, appropriacy, collocation, register, word formation, spelling, and repetition.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- genuinely wide vocabulary is demonstrated repeatedly;
- vocabulary is used fluently and flexibly;
- word choice is consistently precise;
- collocation is natural;
- register is appropriate;
- less common vocabulary is used successfully where useful;
- lexical errors are genuinely occasional/non-systematic;
- noticeable repetition is not a regular feature.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- vocabulary allows some flexibility and precision;
- paraphrasing is generally effective;
- there is repeated evidence beyond basic task language;
- some less common vocabulary is used successfully;
- awkward collocation, imprecision, repetition, spelling, and word-formation problems are limited.

If vocabulary is accurate but mostly ordinary, repetitive, predictable, or template-like, Band 8 is not eligible.
Frequent repetition, generic wording, or several awkward collocations normally point toward Band 6.
Do not reward uncommon words unless they are natural, precise, and correctly collocated.

GRAMMATICAL RANGE AND ACCURACY

Assess both range and accuracy, including sentence variety, clause control, subordination, agreement, tense/aspect, articles, prepositions, pronouns, comparisons, sentence boundaries, and punctuation.

Before scoring, internally scan the whole response for:
- approximate proportion of fully error-free sentences;
- recurring grammar-error families;
- range of structures actually demonstrated;
- control of complex structures;
- punctuation and sentence-boundary control.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- more than half of sentences are fully error-free;
- a genuinely wide range of structures is demonstrated repeatedly;
- complex structures are used flexibly and accurately;
- remaining errors are isolated, not recurring;
- no important grammar weakness is systematic;
- punctuation and sentence boundaries are consistently controlled.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- varied complex structures occur regularly;
- error-free sentences are frequent;
- complex forms are generally controlled;
- sentence patterns show genuine variety;
- recurring errors are limited;
- errors rarely interfere with meaning.

If the same grammar-error family recurs, Band 8 is not eligible.
If multiple recurring grammar weaknesses are present, challenge Band 7 and consider Band 6.
If complex grammar is regularly inaccurate or range is mainly simple/compound, normally do not award above Band 6.
Clear meaning alone is not evidence of Band 7.
