TASK RESPONSE

Assess how fully, directly, relevantly, and sufficiently the learner answers the exact task.

Check:
- Coverage: all explicit parts are addressed.
- Position: when required, it is clear and sufficiently maintained.
- Relevance: main ideas directly answer the task.
- Development: main ideas are explained, reasoned, illustrated, qualified, compared, or otherwise supported.
- Examples: judge mainly for relevance, clarity, plausibility, and argumentative support; do not external fact-check ordinary examples.

Task-type discipline:
- discuss both views + opinion: both views need meaningful treatment and position must be identifiable;
- agree/disagree or extent: degree of agreement must be clear and consistently supported;
- advantages/disadvantages: do not require an outweigh judgment unless asked;
- outweigh: comparative judgment is required;
- causes/problems + solutions: all requested sides must be addressed;
- two direct questions: evaluate each separately;
- positive/negative development: require an appropriate evaluative position.

TASK RESPONSE BAND GATES

Band 8 is eligible only if ALL are true:
- every explicit part is sufficiently addressed;
- the position directly answers the exact question and is consistently maintained;
- the position is well developed, not merely stated;
- every major body idea is directly relevant;
- every major idea is meaningfully explained;
- support/examples clearly advance the reasoning;
- no major idea remains generic, asserted, repetitive, or insufficiently developed;
- there is no substantial digression, memorized filler, or task-general material;
- omissions/lapses are genuinely occasional.

If any condition fails, do not award Band 8.

Band 7 is eligible only if ALL are true:
- all major task parts are addressed;
- the position is clear;
- main ideas are relevant;
- main ideas generally receive explanation/support;
- weaknesses in development are limited rather than characteristic;
- support goes beyond assertion plus example with no explanation;
- task-specific reasoning dominates generic material.

Conservative Task Response caps:
- one major required part omitted -> normally no higher than Band 5;
- one major required part treated only superficially -> normally no higher than Band 6;
- required position unclear, contradictory, or not maintained -> normally no higher than Band 6;
- one part of a two-part task clearly weaker -> Band 8 not eligible; consider Band 6/7 by severity;
- main body ideas mostly asserted with limited explanation -> normally Band 6;
- relevant examples without explanation of significance do not count as full development;
- repetitive conclusions/rephrased claims do not count as development;
- generic or memorized material must not raise Task Response.

The task requires at least 250 words. Do not apply a fixed penalty solely for underlength; judge its effect on coverage/development and evidence of language ability. Do not double-penalize.

COHERENCE AND COHESION

Assess logical organization, progression, paragraph focus, sequencing, relationships between ideas, reference/substitution, and cohesive devices.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- the argument is easy to follow throughout;
- paragraph purpose is consistently clear;
- ideas progress logically within and between paragraphs;
- relationships between claims, explanations, examples, and consequences are clear;
- cohesion is natural and unobtrusive;
- reference/substitution are effective;
- there is no recurring mechanical linking or abrupt reasoning jump.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- progression is clearly maintained;
- paragraphing is logical and purposeful;
- cohesive devices show some flexibility;
- reference/substitution are generally clear;
- lapses are limited.

Formulaic structure with mechanical linkers but understandable progression normally points toward Band 6.
Repeated logical jumps, weak paragraph focus, or loosely connected support normally prevent Band 7.
A standard four-paragraph structure alone is not evidence of Band 7 or 8.

LEXICAL RESOURCE

Assess range, flexibility, precision, appropriacy, collocation, register, word formation, spelling, and repetition.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- genuinely wide vocabulary is repeatedly demonstrated;
- vocabulary is fluent, flexible, and precise;
- collocation is natural;
- register is consistently appropriate;
- less common vocabulary is used successfully and purposefully;
- lexical inaccuracies are genuinely occasional;
- repetition is not a regular feature.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- vocabulary allows some flexibility and precision;
- paraphrasing is generally successful;
- task-specific vocabulary is natural;
- some less common language is used successfully;
- repetition, imprecision, spelling, word-formation, and collocation problems are limited.

If vocabulary looks academic but is formulaic, repetitive, vague, or awkwardly collocated, Band 8 is not eligible.
Frequent basic repetition or generic vocabulary normally points toward Band 6.
Memorized academic phrases that do not fit naturally do not count as range.
Rare vocabulary used inaccurately is negative, not positive, evidence.

GRAMMATICAL RANGE AND ACCURACY

Assess range and accuracy, including sentence variety, clause control, subordination, agreement, tense/aspect, articles, prepositions, pronouns, sentence boundaries, and punctuation.

Before scoring, internally scan the whole response for:
- approximate proportion of fully error-free sentences;
- recurring grammar-error families;
- structural variety;
- control of complex sentences;
- punctuation and sentence-boundary control.

Band 8 is eligible only if:
- more than half of sentences are fully error-free;
- a genuinely wide range of structures appears repeatedly;
- complex structures are flexible and accurate;
- remaining errors are isolated/non-systematic;
- no major grammar weakness recurs;
- punctuation and sentence boundaries are consistently controlled.

Band 7 is eligible only if:
- varied complex structures appear regularly;
- error-free sentences are frequent;
- complex forms are generally controlled;
- recurring errors are limited;
- grammar and punctuation are generally well controlled.

If the same grammar-error family recurs, Band 8 is not eligible.
If multiple recurring grammar weaknesses are present, challenge Band 7 and consider Band 6.
If complex sentences frequently contain errors or sentence structure is repetitive, normally do not award above Band 6.
Meaning being understandable is not enough for Band 7.
